Design workshop · open

One understandable improvement loop

The system should remain ambitious without making every useful change cross a maze of product nouns and durable jobs.

Current truth

Immediate lane

A project strategy can be applied or removed quickly. Explicit project scripts and workflows publish immediately.

Durable lane

Broader changes become experiments, authored capability revisions, preflights, activation, and later outcome feedback.

The problem

The authority split is useful, but the user-facing story and internal orchestration may contain more states and jobs than the product needs.

Current state and job map

settled turn → runtime.reflect_turn → no_change | WorkingAdjustment compare-and-swap | Experiment

Experiment → runtime.author_revision → runtime.preflight → protected activation → observing → runtime.outcome_judge → keep | revise | revert

Working adjustments are immutable project strategies with one active binding per project. Experiments and capability revisions are broader durable changes with different authority, evaluation, and recovery requirements. sourceAdjustmentId links the two paths when an experiment grows from a project adjustment.

Promising simplification

Present one user-facing read model: a change. A change may be active only in this project, under evaluation, durable, revised, or reverted. Do not add a durable Change record or merge existing authorities unless observed duplication proves that one responsibility is stored twice.

  • Reflection produces no_change, an immediate narrow change, or a broader experiment.
  • First simplify presentation: join existing records by exact IDs and hide routine job nouns from the normal view.
  • Then test orchestration simplification: compare today's recoverable authoring and preflight jobs with one experiment job that records the same explicit stages.
  • Keep WorkingAdjustment and Experiment separate unless that comparison finds duplicated transitions, ownership, and recovery state.
  • Feedback updates the same change story instead of exposing a separate pipeline.
  • The system records exact evidence, scope, revision, and authority even if the TUI uses fewer nouns.

Questions to settle together

  1. Which changes besides project strategy should be immediate and unilateral?
  2. Which current transitions, records, or recovery paths are genuinely duplicated rather than merely related?
  3. What is the minimum credible evaluation for prompts, skills, routers, and executable tools?
  4. Can authoring and preflight become stages of one durable job while retaining independent retry, recovery, inspection, and immutable evidence?
  5. When is direct user correction sufficient to keep, revise, or revert a change?
  6. Which existing records and migrations must remain readable during simplification?

Things not to simplify away

  • One declared authority for each datum.
  • Exact immutable revisions used by turns and evaluations.
  • no_change as a normal result.
  • Protected permission and authority expansion.
  • Visible evidence, contestability, and reliable revert.
