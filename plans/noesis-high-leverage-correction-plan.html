Noesis · correction plan

Earn the thesis

Noesis has accumulated a large, well-built capability surface without showing that shared experience creates useful new capability. This plan subtracts unproven scope, makes that claim measurable, closes the causal loop end to end, and only then repays the structural debt.

Premise

Noesis does not currently pass its own review. The product doctrine requires every durable learning to cite evidence, name an anticipated future use, and begin at the narrowest plausible scope. The codebase contains a generated-tool runtime with no caller, session tools registered in no turn, three declared-but-unimplemented TUI panes, a stub protected-authority port, and a full evaluation laboratory that has never judged a real turn — all built at maximum scope, up front, on zero evidence. Reflection run over this repository's own development would classify most of it as un-evidenced scope broadening.

The good part is real

Reserve-before-execute with fail-closed ambiguous reservations, unforgeable WeakSet receipts, blind judging with deterministic label swap, and import-scanning boundary tests are genuinely strong. Nothing here proposes discarding them.

The problem is ratio

Machinery for activation, evaluation, and feedback is complete. The single link that makes any of it matter — an activated revision demonstrably changing a later turn — is absent. Capability was built before the thing capability serves.

The fix is order

The existing plan opens with authority unification, which is architecturally correct and product-wrong: it puts more weeks between the project and its first real evidence, which is the scarcest resource and the one principle 9 says should be steering.

What this plan changes about the existing plan

It does not replace the compounding partnership product loop. It corrects its foundation and adds what it is missing. WS1 executes exactly once as HL-11/HL-12 in Phase 2, using the product-loop WS1 section as its complete acceptance contract. Two things happen first: deletion of genuinely unproven scope, and one vertical causal spine that makes the central claim executable and measurable.

Evidence gates product choices, not correctness. Dogfooding begins as soon as the causal spine runs and continues through the correction. Its four-week readout gates posture heuristics, scope/threshold policy, metric interpretation, and claims of product success. It does not delay the frozen-plan authority, single-store cutover, protected-control-plane repairs, or their correctness tests. No phase may add a new dual-write path.

The measurement that does not exist

This plan tests one necessary part of the thesis: whether Noesis turns shared experience into improved agent behavior. There is no metric for that claim anywhere in the documents or the code. Until there is, this part of the product is unfalsifiable and a well-instrumented placebo is indistinguishable from success.

Effect-free paired shadow replay

Parts of the mechanism already exist and are pointed at the wrong target. packages/evals/src/dynamic-lab.ts runs paired baseline/candidate trials with blind judging against generated trial artifacts; it does not execute a frozen foreground request. Reuse its blind labeling, judging, rails, evidence recording, and aggregation behind a separate effect-free foreground replay port. For eligible settled turns, materialize contemporaneous served and baseline arms from the authoritative frozen plan, replay recorded tool results, and judge the pair blind. Never rerun a live effect. 

eligible settled turn (served revision R, baseline B on its frozen plan)
  → enforce sensitivity/provider policy and reserve call/token/cost budget
  → sample by policy (rate-limited, scope-stratified, model-cohorted)
  → materialize R and B from the same immutable turn plan
  → replay both arms with recorded tool results; exclude effectful gaps
  → existing blind judge + existing protected rails
  → rolling paired estimate for R over B, per scope and model cohort
Metric Definition Thesis is alive when
Served-revision win rate Blind-judged paired wins of the served revision over its pinned baseline on contemporaneous replays, reported per scope, model/runtime cohort, and rolling window. Durably above chance with rails passing. At chance, the adaptation is decoration.
Scope leakage rate Share of turns where an adaptation was served whose scope was unrelated to the turn. Trends toward zero. Guards against compounding degenerating into inject-everything.
Context tax Report injected-context tokens, prompt-layer bytes, and marginal served-minus-baseline input tokens separately. Flat or falling. Advantage bought with a monotonically growing prompt is accumulation.
Correction recurrence Rate at which the same correction signature recurs per relevant exposure, with pre/post activation and served revision recorded. Falls after activation. This is the only metric a human directly feels.

Every metric exposes numerator, denominator, exclusions, replay coverage, model/runtime cohort, scope, and time window. Secret turns are never replayed. Private turns require an authorized provider/data handling policy. Redaction creates a different input and is never reported as an exact replay.

A null result is a result. If the win rate sits at chance after a month of real use, that is a genuine finding about the thesis, not a failure of the build, and the product doctrine should be revised rather than the metric. Record it the same way a negative experiment outcome is recorded today.

Phase 0 · Subtract, then instrument

Days, not weeks. Highest leverage per hour in the entire plan. Nothing here requires new architecture.

HL-01 Delete unproven scope

Owns
packages/tool-runtime, packages/policy/src/protected-authority.ts, packages/tui/src/index.ts:30 pane union, and generated-tool-only satellite contracts, exports, tests, and manifest entries proven unreachable from production
Change
Remove the generated-tool runtime, the unwired protected-authority stub port, the declared-but-unrendered memory / evaluations / jobs panes, and only adapters proven unreachable by a production symbol/import scan. Preserve AgentToolDescriptor and runtime-pi/session-tool-registration.ts: real role execution and the later frozen-plan serving path consume those seams. Git history retains deleted generated-tool work for a future evidence-backed restoration.
Why
Roughly a fifth of the surface exists to satisfy a specification section rather than a caller. It costs review attention, typecheck time, refactor blast radius, and — worst — it makes the repository look further along than it is. The doctrine already forbids adding an abstraction without a consumer in this iteration; this applies it retroactively.
Accept
pnpm check passes with no test deletions other than tests for removed modules. Total production TypeScript falls measurably. No remaining exported module has zero non-test importers.

HL-02 Specify the causal-spine acceptance

Owns
application composition tests, packages/runtime/test/acceptance.test.ts:33-63, and the test-only scripted runtime support
Change
Remove the scripted double's capability-identity echo. Define one decisive acceptance contract at the application composition seam: the immutable plan admitted by WorkspaceStore must be byte-for-byte the plan handed to the execution adapter, and unrelated scope must exclude the adaptation. Add fixture preparation now; land the non-vacuous assertion in the same vertical slice as HL-03 and HL-05, because the current runtime request cannot carry the data required to make it meaningful.
Why
This test is the primary in-repo evidence for the product's central claim, and it is load-bearing in the README. A tautology in that position is worse than no test, because it retires the question.
Accept
A scripted provider response cannot satisfy the assertion by echoing metadata. Completed with HL-03/HL-05: the application test fails when activation is bypassed, immutable bytes are changed, the runtime reconstructs from the legacy registry, or an unrelated turn receives the adaptation.

HL-03 Make the frozen turn plan authoritative

Owns
packages/workspace/migrations/, turn_activation_pins, packages/runtime/src/atomic-activation.ts, and adapter-neutral turn contracts in packages/agent-types
Change
Persist one schema-versioned SQLite-authoritative FrozenTurnPlan before model execution. It contains a stable turn ID; selected capability subset and reason; complete immutable prompt, skill, router, and tool revision references; provider/model/reasoning; permission snapshot; retrieval citations; routing decision; a per-served-revision baseline mapping tied to its experiment/activation; and a canonical digest. Baselines use explicit genesis or unknown_legacy markers. JSONL may reference the plan ID but never duplicate canonical plan data.
Why
A digest authenticates bytes but cannot reconstruct them. Serving, inspection, restart, and replay need the same authoritative, replayable value. This is the minimal SQLite-authority slice required before the full HL-11 cutover and prevents another dual write.
Accept
Admission writes one complete plan atomically. Every served revision has a baseline reference, genesis, or unknown_legacy. Unknown legacy plans remain inspectable but are excluded from causal metrics. The forward-only migration is idempotent and atomic, with verified backup/restore; it does not claim SQL down-migration reversibility.

HL-04 Correct the documents to match the code

Owns
README.md, docs/product-thesis.md, AGENTS.md
Change
Preserve product doctrine and AGENTS invariants as normative. Add one time-bounded known-nonconformities register naming the exact seam, impact, prohibited expansion, and closure task for: raw legacy capability.promoted events affecting serving; protected workspace mutation ports accepting no verifiable receipt; the foreground dual write; and production preflight passing protectedCases: []. Do not weaken each invariant by scattering inline exceptions through doctrine.
Why
These documents are the project's strongest asset and the reason 40k generated lines stayed coherent. An invariant that is documented but unenforced silently converts into an assumption other code relies on. The README already models the right honesty; extend it to the invariants.
Accept
Each shipped nonconformity appears once in the register with a closure task and no permission for new callers or expansion. Normative doctrine remains unqualified.

Phase 1 · The causal spine, then daily use

Goal: the narrowest path on which a correction in one session provably changes a later session, running on your real work every day. Build nothing in this phase that is not on that path. Temporary reliance on the legacy store is acceptable; new dual-write is not.

HL-05 Serve the pinned revision

Owns
new packages/runtime/src/turn-intelligence.ts, apps/noesis/src/runtime-composition.ts, packages/agent-types, and packages/runtime-pi
Change
Complete the HL-02/HL-03 vertical slice. Resolve activation and scope into the authoritative FrozenTurnPlan, then pass that exact plan through the adapter-neutral runtime request. Runtime-pi adapts the plan's turn-scoped tool registrations to Pi; it does not reconstruct product state. The wrapped runtime may not rebuild prompts from capabilities.listActive().
Why
This single missing edge is why the product does not yet exist. Every other subsystem is downstream of it.
Accept
HL-02 passes at the application seam. The plan ID, canonical digest, immutable prompt bytes, and tool revision registrations received by the runtime equal the admitted plan. Missing bytes, digest mismatch, or incompatible permissions fail before model execution. Generated-tool execution remains out of scope after HL-01; prompt and existing session-tool serving still prove the identity path.

HL-06 Genesis baseline so a fresh workspace can learn

Owns
apps/noesis/src/runtime-composition.ts:492
Change
Add an idempotent protected bootstrap that publishes one complete, immutable, file-backed general-collaboration baseline manifest, activates it, and rehydrates it independently of generated experiment manifests. Remove the serving.length === 0 early return that currently makes a fresh workspace incapable of entering reflection.
Why
Today the first correction a new user makes is recorded and discarded. The loop cannot start from an empty activation, which means it cannot start at all.
Accept
A correction on the first turn of a fresh home queues reflection against the immutable baseline. Repeated startup is idempotent; deleting generated experiment fixtures does not make the baseline unresolvable.

HL-07 Move turn settlement out of the composition root

Owns
new packages/runtime/src/turn-settlement.ts, apps/noesis/src/runtime-composition.ts:443-548
Change
Extract the runTurn wrapper into a tested runtime module. Stop monkey-patching the runtime object by spread-and-override. Move correction detection out of the inline regex at line 460 into a pure, tested function in packages/learning — it is a product decision about what counts as user dissatisfaction, and it currently lives in the app as five characters of regex.
Why
The most consequential integration point in the system has one test and is expressed as a patch over another object, so callers cannot tell from a type what they received.
Accept
Composition constructs and injects; it holds no turn logic. Settlement has direct tests for accepted, corrected, failed, and aborted paths. Abort is durably distinguishable—either a dedicated outcome or failed with aborted: true— and is excluded from reflection and replay by default.

HL-08 Real reflection on the controlled Pi path

Owns
credential-free controlled Pi fixtures and application acceptance tests
Change
The original scripted role runner returned no_change for the reflector and throws for every other role, so author, case generator, trial, and judge are never exercised in the path a user actually runs. Script deterministic outputs for the full role set so the controlled Pi journey must traverse reflect, author, evaluate, activate, serve, and protected revert through AgentHarness. Keep this scenario in application acceptance code, not a production demo or runtime mode.
Why
Package acceptance tests prove the organs in isolation. Nothing proves they compose in the application. Until the controlled Pi path can complete a loop, no regression in the loop is detectable.
Accept
A credential-free application acceptance test from an empty home completes a correction, waits for control-plane idle, inspects atomic activation, proves related use and unrelated abstention, and performs a protected revert with no credentials.

HL-09 Start dogfooding and let it hurt

Owns
you
Change
Begin using Noesis as the primary interface for real work against the Pi runtime. Keep a friction log outside the system so the system cannot filter it. Record a four-week readout, but let correctness and architecture work continue during the window.

HL-10 Shadow baseline replay and the compounding read model

Owns
a new effect-free foreground replay port, selected reusable machinery in packages/evals/src/dynamic-lab.ts, and new packages/runtime/src/compounding-metrics.ts
Change
Implement the measurement above without treating a generated TrialArtifact as a foreground replay. Enforce eligibility from all plan provenance: never replay secret data; require authorized provider/data handling for private data; replay recorded tool results or exclude the turn; never rerun effects. Reserve call, token, and cost budget durably before every role with stable operation IDs. Persist exclusion reasons, coverage, and paired R/B evidence.
Why
It makes the thesis falsifiable, and it does so by re-aiming machinery that is already built and already tested rather than by adding a subsystem.
Accept
The read model is live as soon as eligible turns exist. A seeded regression lowers paired win rate. An unrelated-scope injection raises scope leakage. Token and byte taxes are separate. Correction recurrence uses signatures per relevant exposure. Every result reports numerator, denominator, exclusions, coverage, scope, cohort, and window.

Phase 2 · One authority

HL-11/HL-12 execute WS1 from the product-loop plan exactly once. Correctness proceeds while dogfooding runs; the full WS1 acceptance contract, corrected below, is the only completion gate.

HL-11 Collapse the dual store

Owns
packages/runtime/src/index.ts, packages/ledger, packages/workspace/src/importer.ts, apps/noesis/src/runtime-composition.ts
Change
Make SQLite the sole authority for sessions, turns, messages, outcomes, pins, grants, reservations, completions, receipts, and budgets. Introduce a versioned operational cutover protocol separate from the existing source-import marker. Import the full protected graph transactionally, write the cutover marker last, and keep JSONL operational writes active if any step fails. After success, stop appending operational state to JSONL and retain it only as historical provenance. Rehydrate receipt lineage and completion state, not process-local WeakSet objects.
Why
Every turn currently writes both stores with no transaction linking them, so a crash between them produces divergence with no reconciliation path. Two job writers share one jobs table with different semantics. This is the largest structural debt and it grows per turn.
Accept
The corrected full WS1 acceptance set: fault before admission produces no visible turn; fault after admission leaves one unambiguous running turn with its frozen plan; a settled turn produces no legacy append; restart preserves budgets, rejects idempotency-key collisions, replays completed operations without re-running effects, and treats an unresolved reservation as fail-closed. Tests cover an already-imported v1 home, failure before and after marker write, malformed history, completed and failed effect replay, unresolved reservations, and backup/restore. Passing this contract marks product-loop WS1 complete; it must not run again.

HL-12 Retire the legacy promotion path

Owns
packages/capabilities/src/index.ts:163,247
Change
Delete ledger-derived capability promotion. Atomic activation becomes the only way a capability serves a turn. In the same cutover, retire or reroute TUI /promote and /rollback commands through a narrow runtime command port; no UI path may call the legacy registry or raw ledger.
Why
Two promotion systems mean the protected one can be correct while the product uses the other. Guarding both costs more than deleting one.
Accept
No code path or retired command can promote without an activation commit. Appending capability.promoted to the ledger has no effect on serving. Application regression tests exercise both the synthetic event and the retired command path.

Phase 3 · Protection by construction

Today the chokepoint is not a chokepoint. Authority is genuinely unforgeable at the receipt layer — closure-private WeakSet membership is a good mechanism. But the storage ports beneath it accept calls with no receipt at all. Anyone holding NoesisWorkspaceStore can call protectedActivations.commit directly; pin and veto mutate durable control state with no authority wrapper (packages/capabilities/src/atomic.ts:464-516). The invariant is real one layer up and imaginary where the bytes land.

HL-13 Narrow the protected runtime seam

Owns
packages/workspace/src/types.ts, packages/capabilities/src/atomic.ts, and a deep protected-runtime adapter
Change
Capture raw protected workspace ports inside one deep adapter and expose operation-shaped controllers for activation, outcome, pin, veto, and revert. Extend AuthorityBoundary with the matching operations. Do not classify all definition publication as protected: criteria and learning briefs remain declarative; only live protected mutation and active-definition promotion use the protected seam.
Why
Narrowing capability first prevents a receipt parameter from becoming security theater on a broad store that cannot verify it.
Accept
Ordinary workspace, generated-role, and TUI callers cannot name raw protected ports. Only the protected adapter holds them, and the adapter exposes no generic mutation method.

HL-14 Verify receipts at the deep adapter

Owns
protected-runtime adapter, AuthorityBoundary, and receipt verification tests
Change
Require an AuthorityReceipt for every live protected mutation and verify its issuing boundary, effect, resource, and operation before calling the captured raw port. Keep receipt authenticity closure-private; do not make a structurally typed receipt or WorkspaceStore method pretend it can verify itself.
Why
The receipt matters only where the verifier and the mutation are inseparable. The deep adapter provides that locality.
Accept
Forged, foreign-boundary, wrong-effect, wrong-resource, and stale receipts fail without mutation. A valid matching receipt commits exactly once. Architecture tests prove ordinary consumers cannot bypass the adapter.

Correctness does not wait

HL-11/HL-12 begin when the causal-spine contract is green. The four-week dogfood readout gates posture heuristics, learning scope/threshold policy, metric interpretation, and validation claims—not the single-authority cutover.

Hard gate during P1

No task may introduce a new dual-write path. Riding the existing legacy store is temporarily acceptable; widening it is not.

Parallelism

HL-01 and HL-04 are independent. HL-02/03/05 are one serial vertical slice; HL-06/07/08 branch once its contracts stabilize. HL-09 and HL-10 then run alongside correctness work. P4 tasks are mutually independent after the protected/TUI cutovers they consume.

Verification

pnpm format
pnpm lint
pnpm typecheck
pnpm test

The final gate remains pnpm check. Required additions to the focused test classes:

  • served-plan identity: the authoritative plan ID, digest, immutable prompt bytes, and tool revisions equal what the runtime received, and an unrelated turn's plan excludes the adaptation;
  • genesis: a first-turn correction on an empty workspace queues reflection against the immutable baseline;
  • turn settlement: accepted, corrected, failed, and aborted paths tested directly rather than through composition;
  • protected seam: forged, foreign, wrong-effect, and wrong-resource receipts cannot reach a raw protected mutation;
  • protected-case efficacy: a candidate overfitted to its source cases fails the held-out suite;
  • judge inconclusive calibration on an ambiguous fixture;
  • compounding metrics: a seeded regression lowers paired win rate; an unrelated-scope injection raises leakage; all denominators, exclusions, and coverage remain inspectable.
Standing rule. A test whose assertion a scripted provider response can satisfy without the mechanism under test being present is not evidence. HL-02 exists because that rule was violated in the most important test in the repository; apply it to every test added by this plan.

Non-goals

  • Rewriting the control plane. The reserve, receipt, and rail mechanisms are good and stay.
  • Rebuilding the evaluation laboratory. It gets re-aimed at real turns, not replaced.
  • Adding statistical ceremony, judge panels, or new aggregation policy before HL-10 produces evidence that the current rule is wrong.
  • Restoring the generated-tool runtime until a foreground turn genuinely needs it and can pay for real isolation.
  • Any new interface, channel, or persona expansion.
  • New abstractions without a caller in the phase that introduces them. This is the failure being corrected; repeating it inside the correction would be the worst outcome available.

Definition of done

The dogfood protocol is running and its evidence remains an explicit gate on evidence-dependent product decisions. A correction made in one session demonstrably changes a later relevant session and demonstrably does not change an unrelated one. The served plan is the inspected plan. Every new turn has one SQLite authority. No protected mutation compiles without a verifiable receipt at the deep protected adapter. The four compounding metrics are live over eligible real turns, with honest coverage and exclusions. The four-week readout may still conclude that the central claim is unsupported; product-loop decisions that depend on it wait for that evidence.

The success condition is not that the win rate is high. It is that the number exists and is trustworthy. A well-measured null result would still be the most valuable thing this repository has produced.
